# Candidate selection

This study was selected to provide an independent statistical research
question, separate from the companion inventory study. The target is a
published Monte Carlo experiment with explicit distributions and interval
formulas. Its data are generated from those formulas, rather than substituted
for unavailable original observations.

| Candidate | Primary evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Hesterberg (2015), bootstrap interval calibration | [Author's page](https://www.timhesterberg.net/bootstrap-and-resampling), [published article](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2015.1089789), [extended methods](https://arxiv.org/html/1411.5279v1) | Selected: explicit simulation, exact numerical anchor, seven methods, feasible CPU calculation. |
| Gelman and Imbens (2019), high-order polynomials in regression discontinuity | [Author-hosted article](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/2018_gelman_jbes.pdf), [Albada's existing replication and extension (2025)](https://doi.org/10.18718/81781.50) | Excluded: the original panel is already reproduced in a published replication. |
| Long and Ervin (2000), heteroscedasticity-consistent standard errors | [Author's research page](https://jslsoc.sitehost.iu.edu/research_hccm.htm), [published article](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2000.10474549) | Excluded from this run: the linked detailed source could not be retrieved reliably, leaving protocol details unresolved. |

The selected target is journal Figure 10 at n=10,20,40 for normal and
exponential populations, supplemented by all 20 printed cells of Table 5
in the extended manuscript. We include every interval family in that
comparison and retain conditioning, numerical diagnostics, Monte Carlo
uncertainty and a finite-resample sensitivity experiment. Exact ordinates
of the source's plotted curves are unavailable as a numerical table;
comparison therefore separates the deterministic targets, nominal
calibration and the published qualitative conclusions.

The duplicate search was bounded to the original title, author and arXiv
identifier on the ReScience publication and submission sites. It found no
matching Hesterberg replication. A future submitter should repeat that
check and confirm the absence of close collaboration with the original
author before submitting.
